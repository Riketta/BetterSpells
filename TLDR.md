# Better Spells

Autocasts click-and-forget spells and reports ready cooldowns, so no charge is wasted. Everything is optional - nothing runs until you turn it on.

## What it does

- Eligible spells get a toggle button next to their cast button, just like the deathrest auto-wake button; while on, the spell is queued automatically the moment its cooldown finishes - as an ordinary queued job, so sleep, current work and your own orders are never interrupted.
- Only click-and-forget spells qualify: self-buffs and auras that need no target. Targeted spells, ritual starters and confirmation-dialog casts are never autocast. The toggle is shared by all selected pawns, and autocasting pauses during mental breaks.
- When an ability's cooldown completes, a dismissable letter arrives with a per-pawn breakdown. Very short cooldowns are skipped (configurable threshold, default 12 hours).
